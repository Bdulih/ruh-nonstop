What this is

Every airport with scheduled nonstop passenger service to or from Riyadh King Khalid International (RUH), plotted on a Miller cylindrical projection and filterable by great-circle distance.

Where the flight times come from

They are not cached from schedule sources — those are precisely the numbers that disagree with each other (Riyadh–Cairo comes back as 1h58, 2h40 or 3h12 depending on who you ask, and 133, 157 or 202 flights a week). Instead each time is derived from great-circle distance: a fixed half-hour on the ground for taxi, climb and descent, plus cruise at 780 km/h block speed.

hours = 0.5 + km / 780

That is deterministic, so it never goes stale. It is accurate to roughly ±15% — it checks out at RUH–JED 1h35 (typically 1h45), RUH–CAI 2h34 (2h35), RUH–LHR 6h50 (6h45) — but it is an estimate of a typical nonstop, not a timetable. It knows nothing about winds, routings or the specific aircraft.

How often the flights go

It is a weekly count, not a timetable. It will not tell you that your Tuesday flight exists — only that the route runs, say, three times in a normal week. of the destinations here have one; the rest had no figure that could be pinned to the Riyadh route specifically, so they show nothing rather than a guess.

Where the fares come from

Fares were sampled from live queries on , one anchor route per band, and the band is applied to every route in that band. They are ranges for a distance band, not quotes for a route, they are return economy, and they will drift — fares move faster than schedules do. Treat them as an order of magnitude and use the live link for anything real.

Routes flown by a low-cost carrier are flagged, because that moves the real fare more than distance does at the short end.

Coordinates & geometry

  • Airport coordinates: OpenFlights airports.dat, which has not been meaningfully updated since ~2017. Airports missing from it () use coordinates from Wikidata property P625 instead — never guessed.
  • Country boundaries: world-atlas countries-110m (Natural Earth 1:110m), decoded from TopoJSON in-page.
  • Distance: haversine, R = 6371.0088 km. Reference values — RUH–JED 852, RUH–DXB 873, RUH–CAI 1612, RUH–LHR 4941 km.
  • Projection: Miller cylindrical, y = 1.25·ln(tan(π/4 + 0.4φ)), latitude clamped to ±84°.
  • Routes are drawn as true great circles (48-point spherical interpolation), so they bow poleward — that is the actual shortest path, not a drawing error.
  • At 1:110m there is no polygon for , so those countries appear as a dot with no landmass. Not a bug.

Known limits

  • Schedules change constantly. A route listed here may be suspended tomorrow; a new one may not appear for weeks.
  • Seasonal routes are flagged but the season itself is not modelled — a summer-only route is shown year-round.
  • Routes with a future start date are flagged soon and excluded from the counts by default only if you filter them out; they are drawn on the map.
  • Codeshares are excluded. Only airlines that actually operate the metal are listed.
  • No cargo-only routes.
